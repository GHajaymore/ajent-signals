The method

One idea, applied with discipline: fade the extreme.

Markets that stretch too far, too fast tend to snap back. Ajent Pulse looks for that stretch — a short-term oversold dip while the longer trend is still up (or an overbought pop while the trend is down) — using a Connors-style RSI-2 core with Bollinger, RSI-14, CCI and trend filters layered on top. When nothing lines up, it says “No trade” and stays out.

Where most signal apps lie — and we don’t

We don’t sell you a win rate. We show you the math and the log.

A big “90% win rate” badge is easy to print and means almost nothing. What matters is whether the winners outweigh the losers over time. Here’s how we keep ourselves honest.

Tight targets win often but need a high hit-rate; wide targets win rarely but pay more. A high win rate alone proves nothing — the reward-to-risk sets the bar.
Every signal is auto-logged — nothing hidden

Turn on paper trading and Ajent opens and closes each qualifying signal in a virtual account at live market prices. You see the real profit factor, expectancy, win rate, streaks and drawdown — losers included. We can’t cherry-pick, because the app tracks it, not us.

Profit factor > a shiny win rate

The dashboard leads with profit factor (gross wins ÷ gross losses) and expectancy (average result per trade) — the numbers that actually tell you whether an edge exists. Above 1.0 profit factor means the wins outweighed the losses.

Backtested, never promised

The daily swing engine was tested on roughly a decade of US-index data before shipping. We built an ML win-probability model too — and left it out, because out-of-sample it didn’t beat the base strategy. Honest beats impressive.
