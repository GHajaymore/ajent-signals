## Full description (max 4000)
Ajent Signals is an educational markets companion for futures, FX, index and
crypto traders who want to study signals — not chase hype.

WHAT IT DOES
• Live technical read on 40+ global markets — US & world index futures, major FX
  pairs, energy, metals, rates and crypto.
• A clear BUY / SELL / NO-TRADE verdict per market, built from a weighted blend
  of trend, momentum, structure, volatility, volume and news factors.
• Automatic paper trading: the app trades its own signals with virtual money so
  you can watch, in dollars, how they actually perform.
• Every position sizes risk consistently, so results are comparable across
  markets and currencies — no real funds, no broker.

HONEST BY DESIGN
Most signal apps hide their losers and advertise fake win rates. We don't.
Ajent shows the complete, unedited record of every signal — wins and losses.
Short-term markets are close to random, so the goal is disciplined study and
capital protection, not guaranteed profit.

BUILT FOR LEARNING
• Break down each verdict by factor group to understand the "why".
• Charts from intraday to multi-week.
• Pick exactly which markets auto paper-trade, and favourite the ones you follow.
• An in-app explainer of how dollar profit & loss is calculated for any market.

IMPORTANT
Ajent Signals is for education and information only. It is not financial,
investment or trading advice, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy
or sell any security. Trading involves substantial risk of loss. Past results —
including paper-trading results — do not guarantee future performance. Always do
your own research and consult a licensed professional.
